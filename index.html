What it does

Stop logging in to check.

An unofficial Fallout 76 stash tracker for Android. Type what you are hunting for and see which account, which character and how many.

Search everything
One search across every character and every account. Filter by Weapons, Armor, Apparel, Food/Drink and more.
Characters and accounts
Up to five characters per account. Tag each as Main, Alt or Trading MULE and see level, type and entries at a glance.
Your currencies
Caps, Gold Bullion, Stamps and Legendary Scrips are tracked for each character. Atoms are tracked for each account. The home screen shows a combined total of Caps across all your characters.
Trade list builder
Keep For trade and Wanted lists, then tap Copy list for an H: / W: post ready to paste.
Notes and wishlist
Mark the items you are hunting for, with notes like "for ammo crafting".
Yours, on your phone
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your device. Back up and export when you want to.

Get the app

Help test it on Android.

Google needs 12 testers for 14 days before an app can go live. Takes about two minutes.

  1. Join the tester group. groups.google.com/g/mule-app-testers
  2. Opt in. Open the test page on the same Google account and tap Become a tester.
  3. Install from Google Play. If it says not available, wait 15 to 30 minutes and try again.

Please don't tap Buy on the unlock while testing. For testers it is a real charge.
